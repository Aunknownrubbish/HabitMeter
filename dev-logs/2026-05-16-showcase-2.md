## Product Framing

The documentation frames HabitMeter as a living-location decision assistant, not just a map or POI query tool. The main story is:

1. MVP: get map / POI / commute data.
2. P0: make the project reliable and safe.
3. P1: convert raw data into judgments.
4. P2: support real multi-candidate decisions.
5. Showcase: make the work understandable and demonstrable.
